Major Roadmap

At least ten courses across five categories. Here is what counts, when to take it, and what to watch for along the way.

How the ten add up

+Lab
I

Foundation

The empirical toolkit. All three are required.

PSYC 0105Introduction to Psychology
PSYC 0201Psychological Statistics
PSYC 0202Research Methods in Psychology

Timing: finish 0201 and 0202 by the end of sophomore year, and no later than the end of junior year.

II

Core

Pick 3 of these 5 areas, one course from each.

  • ClinicalPsychological Disorders 0224
  • CognitiveCognitive Psychology 0227, Emotions 0205
  • DevelopmentalAdolescence 0216, Child Development 0225
  • PhysiologicalBrain and Behavior 0226, Sensation and Perception 0303
  • Social / PersonalitySocial 0203, Personality 0204, Cultural 0220

Timing: strongly encouraged to finish core courses by the end of junior year.

III

Lab

At least one course with a lab section, on top of Statistics and Research Methods.

It can count as a core or elective at the same time. Look for the lab designation in course descriptions.

IV

Advanced Seminar

One 0400-level seminar, taken junior or senior year.

Seminars focus on synthesizing and integrating theory across the field.

V

Electives

Three more PSYC courses of your choice, winter term included.

One may be independent research: 0350, 0500, 0700, or the 0701/0702/0703 thesis sequence.

A suggested path

  1. First year

    Start with PSYC 0105. It is the gateway to most of the curriculum.

  2. Sophomore

    Take the 0201 then 0202 sequence and begin core courses. Directed Research (0350) is an option with faculty permission.

  3. Junior

    Wrap up core courses and the stats/methods sequence. Thinking about honors? Submit the Thesis Intent Form by early to mid-March.

  4. Senior

    Complete your seminar. Optional: Senior Research (0700) or an honors thesis (0701, 0702, 0703).

Good to know

Research, honors, test scores, transfer credit, and the minor, in one place.

Independent research

Faculty permission is required for all of these.

Sophomores and juniorsDirected Research (0350) or Advanced Research (0500)
SeniorsSenior Research (0700) or Senior Thesis (0701/0702/0703)

Limit: one independent research course (0350, 0500, or 0700) per semester.

Departmental honors

A year-long empirical research project across three terms.

FallPSYC 0701
WinterPSYC 0702
SpringPSYC 0703
  • Submit a Thesis Intent Form by early to mid-March of junior year; talk with a faculty member before then.
  • Minimum 3.5 GPA in psychology courses for honors candidacy.
  • February entrants follow an adjusted schedule.

AP and IB scores

Started Fall 2025 or later: you must take PSYC 0105. Test scores can't waive it.

Started before Fall 2025: AP 4–5 or IB HL 6–7 can bypass 0105, but only if the score is on file with Middlebury before you enroll in a course that requires 0105.

No one can bypass PSYC 0201 (Psychological Statistics) with a test score.

The minor is closing

As of Fall 2025, the psychology minor can no longer be declared.

Interested in psychology alongside another field? See the Neuroscience major or Environmental Studies with a focus in psychology.
